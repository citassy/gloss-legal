Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gloss Books ("Gloss", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses and shares your personal information when you use the Gloss mobile app (the "App").

Gloss Books is operated by Mai Phuong Vu, an individual based in Hungary, who is the data controller for the personal information described here. You can contact us at any time at team.gloss.books@gmail.com.

In short

  • Your book files stay on your device. We only sync details about your books, like title, author and cover.
  • Your notes, chats and reactions are only visible to you and the friends you choose to share with.
  • We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time in the App (Profile → Delete account).

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address, used to sign in with a one-time code.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google: if you choose one of these, we receive basic profile information from Apple or Google, such as your name and email address. With Apple, you can choose to hide your email address, in which case we receive a private relay address.
  • Profile: the display name and username you choose, and an optional profile photo, which you can pick from your photo library or take with your camera.

Your library

  • Book files stay on your device. The EPUB files you add are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • To sync your library across your devices, we store information about each book: title, author, cover image, other basic details from the file, and a digital fingerprint of the file (a code that lets your other devices recognise the same book, without containing its text).

Your reading activity

  • Reading progress, your position in each book, which pages you have read, and whether and when you finished a book.
  • Your reader settings, such as theme, font and text size.
  • If you use reading statistics features, the time you spend reading each day.

Your content

  • Notes and highlights, including the quoted passage, its position in the book and the colour you choose.
  • Chat messages you send about notes, and emoji reactions.

Friends and sharing

  • Friend requests and friendships, which notes and books you share and with whom, and the people you have blocked.

Reports

  • If you report a note, message or person, we store the reason you choose, any details you add, and a copy of the reported content so we can review it.

Notifications

  • In-app notifications (for example friend requests, shared notes and replies).
  • If you allow push notifications, a push notification token for your device.

Technical information

  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, time and type of request) for security and to keep the service running.
  • The App can keep a small diagnostic log on your device to help us fix bugs. It stays on your device unless you choose to share it with us.

We do not use advertising, third-party analytics or tracking tools, and we do not collect your precise location, contacts, health or financial information.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To provide the App's features: your library, reading progress, notes and highlights, and syncing them across your devices.
  • To let you connect with friends, share notes, chat and react.
  • To send you notifications about activity in the App.
  • To keep Gloss safe: to review reports, enforce our Terms and Conditions, and prevent abuse, fraud and security problems.
  • To respond to your messages and support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal bases (EU and UK users)

  • Contract: most of our processing is necessary to provide the App you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping Gloss safe and secure, reviewing reports and preventing abuse.
  • Consent: push notifications and access to your camera and photo library. You can withdraw consent at any time in your device's settings.
  • Legal obligation: when the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

4. Who can see your information in the App

  • Your profile: other Gloss users can find you by your name or username and see your display name, username and profile photo, so they can send you a friend request. People you have blocked cannot find you.
  • Your notes: only you can see your notes, unless you share them. Friends you share a note with can see it, including the quoted passage, along with your name and photo.
  • Chats: a conversation about a note is visible only to you and the one person you are talking with.
  • Reactions: people who can see a note can see how many reactions it has. The note's author can see who reacted.
  • Nothing you create in Gloss is published publicly or shown to people you have not shared it with.

5. Service providers we share information with

We use a small number of service providers to run Gloss. They process information only on our behalf and under their own security and data protection commitments:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage (profile photos and book covers) and server functions. Our data is hosted in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications and App Store distribution.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, and our support email inbox.
  • Resend: sends us an email when a user submits a report, including the report's details.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU, for example in the United States. Where that happens, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the safety of our users or others, or as part of a transfer of the App to a new owner (in which case this policy would continue to apply to your information).

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. How long we keep your information

  • We keep your information for as long as you have an account.
  • When you delete a note or other content, it is removed from the App, including for the friends you shared it with.
  • When you delete your account, your information is removed from our active systems straight away. Copies in backups are deleted automatically within a limited period.
  • Reports, including the copy of the reported content, are kept after an account is deleted, with the link to the account removed, for as long as needed to protect our users and meet legal obligations.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the App: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, profile photo, library information, reading progress, notes and highlights, chats, reactions, friendships and notifications. Notes you shared with friends are removed from their books too. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Gloss's access to your Apple ID.

Book files on your device are not affected; you can remove them by deleting the App.

8. How we keep your information safe

We use encrypted connections, access rules that limit each user to their own data and the data shared with them, and providers with strong security practices. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the authorities of a data breach where the law requires it.

9. Children

Gloss is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you are under 18, you must have your parent's or guardian's permission to use Gloss. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU, UK and several US states), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your information;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it.

You can update most of your information and delete your account directly in the App. For any other request, email team.gloss.books@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time the law requires. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

If you are in the EU or UK and believe we have not handled your information properly, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In Hungary, this is the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, naih.hu).

11. Device permissions

  • Photos and camera: only used when you choose or take a profile photo.
  • Notifications: used to tell you about friend requests, shared notes and replies.
  • Files: used when you choose an EPUB file to add to your library.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device's settings.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Gloss changes. We will update the date at the top, and if the changes are significant, we will let you know in the App or by email before they take effect.
